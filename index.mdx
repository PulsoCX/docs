## What Pulso does

Pulso is a SaaS that replaces traditional satisfaction surveys with a conversation. A company (the `tenant`) defines a "moment of truth" once (for example: "the customer just downgraded from Premium", "we just delivered the product", "the support ticket was closed last week") and Pulso reaches the customer through the channel of choice with a tailored conversation, runs an AI agent that adapts in real time, analyzes the transcript, and triggers downstream actions on the tenant's stack.

The integration surface is intentionally narrow: a single REST endpoint starts a conversation, and the platform takes care of identity, channels, AI behavior, analysis and execution.

## How a conversation flows

1. The integrator POSTs to `https://api.pulso.cx/v1/conversations` with the `pulsoVersionId`, the `channel`, and the customer-specific payload (`phoneE164` or `email`).
2. Pulso creates the conversation row and dispatches the outbound action based on the channel:
   - **Phone**: an outbound Twilio call. The AI agent speaks in real time over the line with full-duplex audio.
   - **Email**: a transactional invitation with a signed link to `chat.pulso.cx`. The customer answers on the web chat without re-identifying themselves.
   - **WhatsApp**: the approved `pulso_es` template via Meta Cloud API. The customer answers in WhatsApp and the agent replies in the same thread.
3. The conversation runs until the agent decides it has enough signal or the budget (turns or minutes) is exhausted.
4. The analysis pipeline scores the transcript against the tags defined in the `pulsoVersion` and produces an action plan.
5. The action tools attached to the Pulso fire: CRM updates via Composio, signed webhooks to custom MCP endpoints, follow-up emails to the tenant.
